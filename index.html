Research interests

I work on nonparametric statistics and statistical learning, with a current focus on:

  • Federated learning · 联邦学习
  • Distributed learning and statistical inference · 分布式学习与统计推断
  • Differential privacy · 差分隐私
  • Neural networks and robust regression · 神经网络与稳健回归
  • Conformal prediction · 共形预测
  • Spatial and image data analysis · 空间与图像数据分析

I welcome inquiries about graduate study and research collaboration in statistics and machine learning.

Research overview

My research connects communication-efficient statistical inference with robust nonlinear learning and the analysis of spatial and image data. I am also interested in generative modelling, as part of a broader statistical machine learning programme.
